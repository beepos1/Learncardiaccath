C-arm simulator

Full free play. Rotate (LAO/RAO) and angulate (CRA/CAU), switch between the left and right coronary, inject contrast, and flip to colour-coded anatomy to check what you're looking at.

Keyboard

← → RAO / LAO   ↑ ↓ CRA / CAU
Space inject   L labels   C anatomy colours

Angio vs. Anatomy

Angio mimics the fluoroscopy image, showing only the injected artery. Anatomy colour-codes every vessel so you can check which branch is which.

Try this

Start in AP. Swing to LAO 45 and watch the spine slide to the right of the screen. Now add 30° caudal: the spider appears and the diaphragm drops out of view.
